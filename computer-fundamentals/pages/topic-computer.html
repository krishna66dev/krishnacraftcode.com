What is a Computer?

Topic Explanation
Read in your preferred language

A computer is an electronic device that can receive data (input), process it according to a set of instructions (program), and produce a result (output). The word "computer" comes from the Latin word computare, meaning "to calculate."

Computers work on a simple three-step cycle called the IPO Cycle: Input → Processing → Output. Everything a computer does — from playing a video to solving a math problem — follows this cycle.

A computer is made up of two main parts: Hardware (physical components you can touch, like the keyboard, CPU, and monitor) and Software (programs and instructions that tell the hardware what to do, like Windows or MS Word).

Modern computers can perform billions of calculations per second, store vast amounts of data, and communicate with other computers over networks. They are used in every field — medicine, education, banking, entertainment, and more.

कंप्यूटर एक इलेक्ट्रॉनिक उपकरण है जो डेटा (इनपुट) को ग्रहण करता है, निर्देशों (प्रोग्राम) के अनुसार उसे संसाधित (प्रोसेस) करता है, और परिणाम (आउटपुट) प्रदान करता है। "कंप्यूटर" शब्द लैटिन शब्द computare से बना है, जिसका अर्थ है "गणना करना।"

कंप्यूटर एक सरल तीन-चरण प्रक्रिया पर कार्य करता है, जिसे IPO चक्र कहते हैं: इनपुट → प्रोसेसिंग → आउटपुट। कंप्यूटर की हर गतिविधि — चाहे वीडियो चलाना हो या गणित की समस्या हल करना — इसी चक्र पर आधारित है।

कंप्यूटर दो मुख्य भागों से मिलकर बना है: हार्डवेयर (भौतिक घटक जिन्हें हम छू सकते हैं, जैसे कीबोर्ड, CPU और मॉनिटर) और सॉफ्टवेयर (प्रोग्राम और निर्देश जो हार्डवेयर को बताते हैं कि क्या करना है, जैसे Windows या MS Word)।

आधुनिक कंप्यूटर एक सेकंड में अरबों गणनाएँ कर सकते हैं, बड़ी मात्रा में डेटा संग्रहीत कर सकते हैं, और नेटवर्क के माध्यम से अन्य कंप्यूटरों से संचार कर सकते हैं। इनका उपयोग चिकित्सा, शिक्षा, बैंकिंग, मनोरंजन आदि हर क्षेत्र में होता है।

Key Points to Remember
Important facts for exam preparation
  • A computer is an electronic device — it works on electrical signals.
  • It follows the IPO Cycle: Input → Processing → Output.
  • A computer can only do what it is programmed (instructed) to do — it has no intelligence of its own.
  • It has two main components: Hardware and Software.
  • The brain of a computer is the CPU (Central Processing Unit).
  • Computers work with binary numbers (0 and 1), called bits.
  • The first electronic computer was ENIAC (1945), built in the USA.
  • Charles Babbage is called the "Father of the Computer" for inventing the Analytical Engine.

Real-Life Examples
Relate the concept to everyday life
⌨️
Example 1 — Typing in MS Word

Input: You press keys on the keyboard → Process: CPU converts keystrokes into characters → Output: Text appears on your monitor screen. This is the IPO cycle happening in real time.

🧮
Example 2 — Using a Calculator App

Input: You type "5 + 3" using the keyboard → Process: CPU's ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) performs the addition → Output: "8" is displayed on screen.

🏥
Example 3 — Hospital (Real World)

Input: Patient's data is entered by the doctor → Process: Computer analyzes data using diagnostic software → Output: A medical report is printed. The same IPO model applies!

Key Notes
Important exam tips — don't miss these!
ENIAC (Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer) was the world's first general-purpose electronic computer, built in 1945 at the University of Pennsylvania.
Charles Babbage designed the "Analytical Engine" in 1837 — the concept that became the modern computer. He is called the Father of the Computer.
Ada Lovelace wrote the world's first algorithm for Babbage's engine. She is called the First Programmer.
Computers work in binary (0 and 1) — all data, including text, images, and audio, is ultimately represented in these two digits.
Full form: CPU = Central Processing Unit. It is the "brain" of the computer.
Summary — What is a Computer?

A computer is an electronic device that processes data using the IPO Cycle (Input → Processing → Output). It consists of hardware (physical parts) and software (programs). The CPU is the brain, RAM is temporary memory, and storage devices (HDD/SSD) hold data permanently. Computers work in binary (0s and 1s). Charles Babbage is the Father of the Computer, and ENIAC was the first electronic computer (1945). Computers are used in every field of modern life.

Interview / Viva Questions
Click each question to reveal the answer
1 What is a computer? Give its basic definition.
A computer is an electronic device that accepts data as input, processes it according to a set of instructions (software), and produces the result as output. It can store data and retrieve it when required.
2 What is the IPO Cycle? Explain with an example.
IPO stands for Input → Processing → Output. Example: When you type on a keyboard (Input), the CPU processes the keystrokes (Processing), and the text appears on the monitor (Output).
3 Who is called the "Father of the Computer" and why?
Charles Babbage (1791–1871) is called the Father of the Computer. He designed the "Difference Engine" and the "Analytical Engine" — mechanical devices that were the conceptual ancestors of modern computers.
4 What is ENIAC and what is its significance?
ENIAC (Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer) was the world's first general-purpose electronic digital computer, built in 1945 at the University of Pennsylvania by J. Presper Eckert and John Mauchly.
5 What does CPU stand for and what is its role?
CPU stands for Central Processing Unit. It is the "brain" of the computer that performs all arithmetic, logical, and control operations. It consists of the ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) and the Control Unit (CU).
