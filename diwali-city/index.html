About Diwali
Diwali, the Festival of Lights, celebrates the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. Families light diyas, draw rangoli, share sweets, wear new clothes, and watch fireworks together. Click glowing diyas in the central garden and buildings along the streets to explore.
